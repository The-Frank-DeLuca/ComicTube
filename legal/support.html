Common Questions

  • How do I upgrade to Premium? Go to your account settings and select "Upgrade to Premium" ($6.99/month). Payments are processed securely through Stripe.
  • How do comedians get paid? Comedians connect a Stripe Express account to receive monthly payouts from subscriptions and tips.
  • My video upload failed. What do I do? Try re-uploading the file. If the issue continues, contact us using the email above and include the content title and approximate upload time.
  • How do I report inappropriate content? Use the report button on any post or profile, or email us directly with a link to the content.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Go to account settings and select "Manage Subscription." Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
  • I'm having trouble with Adult Mode / age verification. Adult Mode is only available to verified users 18 and older. Contact support if you believe your access is incorrect.
